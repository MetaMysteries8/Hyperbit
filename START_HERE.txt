HYPERBIT - START HERE

YOU DO NOT NEED TO KNOW HOW TO COMPILE C++.

1. Extract the whole ZIP.
2. Double-click MAKE_THE_HEX.bat.
3. Wait. It installs the required tools and compiles the firmware.
4. When it says DONE, your real .hex is:

   firmware\HyperBit.hex

5. Plug the micro:bit V2 itself into your PC over USB.
6. Double-click FLASH_FIRMWARE.bat.
7. Edit config.cmd with your Hyper API key.
8. Double-click RUN_AGENT.bat.

If MAKE_THE_HEX.bat fails, send ChatGPT a screenshot of the last error lines.
